## How Dropdawg Works

Dropdawg operates as a bridge between customers and restaurants, leveraging technology to streamline the entire food delivery process. Our mobile app features a user-friendly interface that allows you to discover nearby restaurants, place orders, and track deliveries in real-time. Restaurants integrate with our platform to receive orders instantly, prepare meals efficiently, and maintain high-quality standards.
